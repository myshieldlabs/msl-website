01 / About MyShieldLabs

Safety should feel like a right — not a privilege.

We are an early-stage personal safety company. We listen to everyday concerns and explore ways technology can support people with practical, approachable safety experiences.

Our starting point is simple: understand people first, then create solutions around what matters to them.

We believe personal safety products should be considerate, easy to understand and designed for real life. Our work is guided by empathy, user confidence and a commitment to communicate honestly as our ideas develop.

03 / Our first product

Meet HerSafeWalk.

Currently in development

HerSafeWalk is a personal safety wearable being developed with women's everyday experiences in mind. It is being shaped around a simple idea: a discreet way to communicate a safety status and request help, supported by a companion phone experience.

✦A quick way to signalDesigned around a simple, easy-to-reach interaction.
✦Everyday check-insConcepts for communicating when things feel okay or uncomfortable.
✦Discreet to carryA wearable form intended to fit into daily routines.
✦Clear feedbackDesigned to make interactions feel understandable.

05 / Our approach

Thoughtful products start with people.

We are an early-stage team. Our priority is to understand everyday needs, shape a useful experience and develop responsibly.

Understand first

Listen to the people a safety product is meant to serve, and take their everyday concerns seriously.

Keep it simple

Make the experience clear, considerate and easier to understand, especially in stressful moments.

Build trust

Learn from feedback and communicate honestly about what is ready and what is still being developed.

We’re working towards the first version of HerSafeWalk.

HerSafeWalk is currently in development and is not yet available for purchase. We are refining the product experience and working towards validation. Features, availability and final product details may evolve as development progresses.
