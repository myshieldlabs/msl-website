MYSHIELDLABS — MINIMAL PROFESSIONAL WEBSITE

Design concept:
- New minimal editorial layout; no glassmorphism, blur, frosted panels or glass cards.
- Clean typographic hierarchy, generous spacing, thin dividers, strong grid alignment and restrained motion.
- Dark mode is the default, with a proper persistent dark/light theme toggle.
- The official supplied Ice White and Obsidian logos swap automatically with the selected theme.
- Mobile navigation is responsive and keyboard-accessible.

Brand colours:
- Uses only the official 12-colour MyShieldLabs palette.
- Dark mode: Obsidian / Brand Navy / Midnight Navy foundations.
- Light mode: Ice White foundation with dark text.
- Supporting blues are reserved for emphasis, links and actions.

Public information:
- Customer-facing company story, purpose, values, focus, product overview and development status.
- HerSafeWalk is clearly marked as in development and not yet available for purchase.
- No detailed engineering, implementation, components or internal R&D disclosure.
- Contact email: myshieldlabs@gmail.com.
- Social links are placeholders pending official URLs.

Publishing:
1. Extract this ZIP.
2. Upload index.html and the assets folder to your existing GitHub Pages repository.
3. Commit the update.
4. Keep your custom domain and DNS configuration unchanged.
